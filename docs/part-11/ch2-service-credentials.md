## APPENDICES APPENDIX A INTERPRETATIONS OF CHURCH LAW

#### ¶6400 {#p6400}
This section contains the official interpretations of The Discipline of The Wesleyan Church by the General Superintendent which have been sustained by the General Conference and are therefore in full force and effect as Church law (1935). Each interpretation is identified by the paragraph number of The Discipline to which it refers.
Whenever a revision of The Discipline makes an interpretation unnecessary or renders it obsolete, the interpretation is to be deleted from The Discipline by order of the General Conference (Minutes of the 1976 General Conference, proceeding [276](/part-1/ch4-the-constitution-of-the-north-american-general-conference.md#p276)).
