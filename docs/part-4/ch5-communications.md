# Chapter 5 — Communications

#### ¶2100 {#p2100}
Vision. The vision of Communications is to spread hope and holiness that transforms lives and nations by promoting The Wesleyan Church’s Christ-exalting message and biblical values; by making denominational news and information, official views, and mission-aligned resources readily available to its leaders, members, and diverse constituents; and by assisting church personnel with essential creative and editorial support.

#### ¶2105 {#p2105}
Purpose. Communications’ purpose is to pursue this vision through serving the Church as its public voice, image guardian, media liaison; through the
strategic use of electronic, digital, broadcast, video, print, and social media; through creative design, innovative messaging, public relations, resource production, marketing, advertising, writing, editorial services, web design and supervision, reception, directory services, and internal communications.

#### ¶2110 {#p2110}
Executive Director. The Executive Director of Communications is elected by the General Conference as a general official ([334](/part-1/ch4-the-constitution-of-the-north-american-general-conference.md#p334); [360:2](/part-1/ch4-the-constitution-of-the-north-american-general-conference.md#p360-2); 1580:1; 1800), and is an ex officio member of the General Conference ([1503:1c](/part-4/ch1-general-conference.md#p1503-1c)) and the Executive Leadership Band (1990). The Executive Director of Communications is accountable to the General Board, serves as a nonvoting resource person to the board, is under the supervision of the General Superintendent (1935). The Executive Director of Communications shall exercise leadership in this office in keeping with The Discipline, the General Board Policy for Communications, and other directives as may be given by the General Conference, the General Board, or its Executive Board. The term of service and other regulations for this office are provided ([1810–1870](/part-4/ch3-general-officials-of-the-church.md#p1810)).
