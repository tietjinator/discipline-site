# Chapter 6 — Global Partners

#### ¶2258 {#p2258}
Vision. The ultimate objective of The Wesleyan Church is to fulfill the Great Commission of the Lord Jesus Christ by sharing with all people the good news and glorious experience of full salvation.

#### ¶2259 {#p2259}
Purpose. Global Partners is responsible for global evangelism and church multiplication, carrying out the missionary objectives of The Wesleyan Church in those fields and areas beyond the national borders of the United States and Canada, and in such other places as shall be assigned to it as approved by the General Conference or the General Board. This is accomplished through evangelism, church maturation and multiplication, ministerial and leadership training, and new cross-cultural mission sending. Global Partners also functions as a missionary sending agency for non-Wesleyan Network Missionaries who align with The Wesleyan Church’s Articles of Religion (210–250) and Guides and Helps to Holy Living (260–268).

#### ¶2261 {#p2261}
Executive Director. The Executive Director of Global Partners is elected by the General Conference from among the ordained ministers of The Wesleyan Church as a general official, and is an ex officio member of the General Conference ([1503:1c](/part-4/ch1-general-conference.md#p1503-1c)) and the Executive Leadership Band (1990). The Executive Director of Global Partners is accountable to the General Board, serves as a nonvoting resource person to the board, and is under the supervision of the General Superintendent. The Executive Director of Global Partners shall exercise leadership in keeping with The Discipline, the General Board Policy for Global Partners, and other directives as may be given by the General Conference, the General Board, or its Executive Board. The term of service and other regulations for this office are provided for in [1810–1870](/part-4/ch3-general-officials-of-the-church.md#p1810).

#### ¶2262 {#p2262}
Strategic Directives. The strategic directives of Global Partners and its executive director shall be to:

##### ¶2262:1 {#p2262-1}
Evangelize the nations of the earth, seeking the salvation of the individuals and the entire sanctification of believers as the primary responsibility of all missionary work.

##### ¶2262:2 {#p2262-2}
Gather the converts into churches and enlist them in the work of God’s kingdom.

##### ¶2262:3 {#p2262-3}
Give special emphasis to the training of workers and leaders for the Church.

##### ¶2262:4 {#p2262-4}
Share the benefits of the gospel and facilitate evangelistic work through specialized ministries such as medical, educational, literacy, and benevolent work.

##### ¶2262:5 {#p2262-5}
Promote, in accordance with scriptural and indigenous principles, the growth and development of the churches in each field or area to a church body that is spiritually mature, well organized, financially responsible, and missionary minded, and that can assume its place in The Wesleyan Church.

##### ¶2262:6 {#p2262-6}
Further, the specific vision, mission, global strategic plan, core missiological values, and general duties for Global Partners shall be prepared in collaboration with the General Superintendent and be presented to the Executive Leadership Band for ratification and to the General Board for final approval.

**1. Missionaries**

#### ¶2272 {#p2272}
A missionary or other worker duly appointed to serve under Global Partners may retain membership in the local church and district where membership is held as of the time of appointment by the General Board. The missionary or other worker shall also relate to the national church where assigned and shall hold membership as defined in The Discipline or other regulations adopted for the particular field to which assigned and according to the General Board Policy for Global Partners. The missionary or other worker shall be accountable for all conduct to the proper authorities on the field or area of service, the Executive Director of Global Partners, and the General Board as defined in the General Board Policy on Church Discipline and the General Board Policy for Global Partners. The network missionaries shall be amenable for all conduct to the proper authorities on the field or area of service, the Executive Director of Global Partners, and the denomination that appointed them as defined in their governance structure.

**2. Organization of Mission Units**

#### ¶2275 {#p2275}
Mission units under Global Partners shall be organized on three levels:

##### ¶2275:1 {#p2275-1}
A developing district shall be a unit which does not meet the requirements for an established district.

##### ¶2275:2 {#p2275-2}
An established district shall be a unit with a minimum of twenty churches and seven hundred fifty members.

##### ¶2275:3 {#p2275-3}
A national or regional conference shall be a unit with two or more developing or established districts.

#### ¶2278 {#p2278}
Mission units under Global Partners shall be entitled to send representatives to the North American General Conference on a plan of representation determined by the General Board.

#### ¶2281 {#p2281}
Membership Rights. In keeping with the Statement of Relationship (6730) contained in the Essentials (see the Charter of The International Conference of The Wesleyan Church), members of units under Global Partners have the right to transfer membership not only among local Wesleyan churches but also from one unit to any other unit, or to districts within the United States or Canada ([567](/part-2/ch2-membership.md#p567); [2550](/part-5/ch2-general-conferences-and-established-national-regional-conferences.md#p2550)).

#### ¶2284 {#p2284}
Transfer of Ministers. The transfer of ministerial credentials from
one unit under Global Partners to any other unit, or to any district of the United States or Canada, may be effected with the understanding that consideration must be given to differences in requirements for licensing and ordination of ministers as provided for in the various books of discipline of units worldwide ([6730:4a](/part-1/ch1-history.md#p6730-4a)).

#### ¶2287 {#p2287}
Eligibility for Holding Office. Global Partners units are expected to adopt, subject to the approval of the General Board, a discipline or other similar document for the administration of the churches within their boundaries ([2500:5](/part-5/ch1-basic-principles.md#p2500-5)). While the right to be elected to office is worldwide, the right to hold office shall be exercised within the administrative jurisdiction of each book of discipline.
