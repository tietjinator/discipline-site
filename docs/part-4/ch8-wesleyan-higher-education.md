# Chapter 8 — Wesleyan Higher Education

#### ¶2338 {#p2338}
The vision of Wesleyan Higher Education is to spread hope and holiness that transforms lives and nations by promoting The Wesleyan Church’s Christ-exalting message and biblical values by infusing Wesleyan theology and values throughout Wesleyan higher education and by ensuring effective ministerial education, development, and programs.

**2. Purpose**

#### ¶2339 {#p2339}
The purpose of Wesleyan Higher Education is to pursue this vision through serving the Church by coordinating the implementation of The Wesleyan Church’s objectives for Christian education as they relate to the training of Christian ministers, missionaries, and educators on the study course, college, and seminary levels, the development of youth in higher education for various fields of service, and continuing education and career development for the ministers of The Wesleyan Church.

## B. Structure 1. Assistant General Superintendent

#### ¶2340 {#p2340}
The Assistant General Superintendent who is elected by the General Conference from among the ordained ministers of The Wesleyan Church as a general official, is an ex officio member of the General Conference ([1503:1c](/part-4/ch1-general-conference.md#p1503-1c)) and the Executive Leadership Band (1990), and is a member and the executive secretary of both the Committee on Chaplains ([2395](#p2395)) and the Wesleyan Higher Education Council ([2355](#p2355)). The Assistant General Superintendent is accountable to the General Board, serves as a nonvoting resource person to the board, and is under the supervision of the General Superintendent. The Assistant General Superintendent shall exercise leadership in keeping with The Discipline, General Board Policy, and other directives as may be given by the General Conference, the General Board, or its Executive Board. The term of service and other regulations for this office are provided in [1810–1870](/part-4/ch3-general-officials-of-the-church.md#p1810).

**2. Wesleyan Higher Education Council**

#### ¶2355 {#p2355}
The Wesleyan Higher Education Council shall consist of the General Superintendent, who shall have direct oversight of the Wesleyan Higher Education Council, the Assistant General Superintendent, who shall serve as its executive secretary ([2340](#p2340)), and the president and chair of the board of trustees for each higher education institution and the executive vice president and chair of the board of Wesley Seminary ([2365](#p2365) and [2366](#p2366)). The Council shall serve as an advisory body to the General Superintendent.

###### ¶2355:5:c {#p2355-5-c}
C. Higher Education Institutions

###### ¶2355:5:c:1 {#p2355-5-c-1}
1. Ownership and Control

###### ¶2355:5:c:1:a {#p2355-5-c-1-a}
a. Basic Principles

#### ¶2358 {#p2358}
The higher education institutions of The Wesleyan Church shall be governed in harmony with these principles:

##### ¶2358:1 {#p2358-1}
All schools, in fulfilling the mission of The Wesleyan Church, shall seek to produce Christian workers and committed lay members for the church of Jesus Christ. The Church recognizes that more than one type of higher education institution will be needed. Some institutions will devote themselves primarily to the preparation of full-time Christian workers for the church. Some colleges, recognizing the God-given mandate to explore and bring under dominion the whole range of knowledge for the glory of God and the good of mankind (Gen. 1:26–28; 9:1–7; Matt. 6:10; 1 Cor. 3:21b–23; 2 Cor. 10:5; 1 Tim. 4:4–5), will offer a Christian liberal arts program. Some students will use such a curriculum as training for full-time, church-related vocations or as a foundation for graduate training for such vocations; some to prepare for other vocations in which they have been called to serve Christ, his church, and his world.

##### ¶2358:2 {#p2358-2}
The Church and all its schools shall work in the closest harmony. In its legislative assemblies, the Church defines its faith. In its classrooms, the Church studies, expounds, and defends its faith. In the congregation, the Church worships its Lord and proclaims its faith. To safeguard the doctrinal purity of the Church, it shall be required that all schools maintain and promote the doctrinal position of the Church as defined in its Articles of Religion, Guides and Helps to Holy Living, and Elementary Principles. Any person employed on the administrative staff or faculty of an educational institution of The Wesleyan Church must affirm adherence to the doctrine of entire sanctification and other doctrines of The Wesleyan Church Articles of Religion.

##### ¶2358:3 {#p2358-3}
In establishing its higher education institutions, and in guiding their life and work, The Wesleyan Church seeks to provide the highest possible quality of Christian education for its own constituents and for other persons who wish to study under its auspices without regard to race, national origin, or gender ([265:12](/part-1/ch4-the-constitution-of-the-north-american-general-conference.md#p265-12); 360:3d).

**b. Lines of Authority**

#### ¶2362 {#p2362}
Ultimate authority over the higher education institutions of The Wesleyan Church rests with the General Conference ([1590:13](/part-4/ch1-general-conference.md#p1590-13)). In practice this authority is delegated to the General Board and consists of the following:

##### ¶2362:1 {#p2362-1}
Authority, by two-thirds vote, to establish, merge or dissolve an educational institution, or to enter into a cooperative agreement with other persons/groups of like theological and behavioral commitment in the ownership and operation of an higher education institution.

##### ¶2362:2 {#p2362-2}
Authority to provide for and deal with all matters pertaining to charters and bylaws.

##### ¶2362:3 {#p2362-3}
Provision for boards of control in keeping with [340:2](/part-1/ch4-the-constitution-of-the-north-american-general-conference.md#p340-2).

##### ¶2362:4 {#p2362-4}
The adoption and revision of General Board Policy Standards for Higher Education Institutions as the General Board shall deem to be wise.

##### ¶2362:5 {#p2362-5}
The General Board is not empowered or authorized to contravene, override, or set aside a decision of a college board of trustees except as is provided and specifically stated in [2362:1–4](#p2362-1).

#### ¶2368 {#p2368}
Each of the four-year colleges and Wesley Seminary shall maintain a curriculum which satisfies the educational requirements for ordination as minister for The Wesleyan Church.

**3. Financial Support**

#### ¶2377 {#p2377}
The financial support of the higher education institutions of The Wesleyan Church shall be provided for as follows:

##### ¶2377:1 {#p2377-1}
The General Fund (2035). The General Fund shall include provision for Wesleyan Higher Education ([2338](#p2338)), and the Wesleyan Higher Education Council ([2355](#p2355)).

##### ¶2377:2 {#p2377-2}
The Education Fund (2040). The Education Fund shall provide for the support of the approved higher education institutions ([2365](#p2365)), the Ministerial Study Course Agency ([2385–2388](#p2385)), and the subsidizing of ministerial training ([2035:1](/part-4/ch4-general-administration.md#p2035-1)).

##### ¶2377:3 {#p2377-3}
Special Offerings. Each higher education institution shall supplement its income through school representation work, solicitations, and special offerings ([2050:3](/part-4/ch4-general-administration.md#p2050-3)).

## D. Ministerial Study Course Agency

#### ¶2385 {#p2385}
Ministerial training within The Wesleyan Church will ordinarily take place in one of the approved higher education institutions. However, for those persons whose circumstances make such institutional education impossible in whole or in part, the Assistant General Superintendent shall provide other options of study. The correspondence courses shall also be open to others who wish to prepare for Christian service ([3200–3210](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3200)).

#### ¶2388 {#p2388}
The Ministerial Study Course Agency shall be maintained at the denominational headquarters to centralize and standardize the keeping of records on all students for the various ministries recognized by The Wesleyan Church and the administering of the correspondence study courses for those ministries
([3200–3210](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3200)). It shall be directed by the Assistant General Superintendent whose duties shall be to:

##### ¶2388:1 {#p2388-1}
Maintain records on all students for the various ministries recognized by The Wesleyan Church, whether enrolled in higher education institutions or in correspondence courses ([3170:1–3](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3170-1)), and including students of the lay minister’s course of study ([3210:4](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3210-4); [3420](/part-6/ch4-special-lay-ministries.md#p3420)), commissioned minister’s course of study ([3059:1b](/part-6/ch1-ministerial-orders-and-regulations.md#p3059-1b); 3210:3), the pre-ordination course of study ([3040:2](/part-6/ch1-ministerial-orders-and-regulations.md#p3040-2); 3051:1; 3070:3; 3210:1), the courses of study for specialized ministries ([3210:2](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3210-2)), and the special worker’s courses of study ([3210:5](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3210-5); [3460–3470](/part-6/ch4-special-lay-ministries.md#p3460)). The records shall include transcripts, grades, courses finished, and current standing. The Agency shall exchange needed information with the higher education institutions and the local and district boards directing the students, issuing an annual report concerning progress for the year and current standing to each student, a similar report on each student of the lay minister’s course of study to the local board of administration of the local church where membership is held, and a similar report on each student recognized by the district to the home district board of ministerial development prior to the session of the district conference ([1387:2](/part-3/ch6-district-ministerial-supervision.md#p1387-2); 1390:2; 3040:2; 3460).

##### ¶2388:2 {#p2388-2}
Provide a correspondence course for each course required in each course of study, securing the services of a qualified instructor and approving the choice of textbooks and study plans, providing for the registration of students properly authorized to use the correspondence courses ([3170:3](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3170-3)), and providing study guides and instructions as well as examinations which shall be administered through an ordained minister whenever possible ([3200](/part-6/ch2-ministerial-education.md#p3200)).

##### ¶2388:3 {#p2388-3}
Provide suitable transcripts and issue certificates upon the satisfactory completion of the work assigned ([6360](/part-11/ch2-service-credentials.md#p6360)).

##### ¶2388:4 {#p2388-4}
Evaluate any training taken outside the institutions and agencies of The Wesleyan Church by a student for one of the ministries recognized by The Wesleyan Church, to judge the equivalence of such outside training to the appropriate course of study, and to report any conclusions and recommendations to the appropriate local board of administration or district board of ministerial development ([3070:4](/part-6/ch1-ministerial-orders-and-regulations.md#p3070-4); 3104:4; 3170:4; 3470).

## E. Committee on Chaplains

#### ¶2395 {#p2395}
The Committee on Chaplains shall include the Assistant General Superintendent as its executive secretary, with other members to be selected as provided by the General Board.
